Create a beautiful, modern homepage. Give it a friendly headline welcoming people to personalized math tutoring for middle schoolers. Right below the headline, put two big, clear buttons: [Create a New Account] and [Log In to Existing Account].

When someone tries to create an account, enforce these strict rules right on the screen:
